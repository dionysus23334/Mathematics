## Citation Conventions

- **Rudin route**: Chapters are labeled "Rudin Chapter n", but the exposition and proofs are rewritten according to the site's own prior development and are not translated verbatim.
- **Wikipedia**: Used to verify definitions, theorem names, and standard formulations; the main text relies primarily on textbook-style proofs.
- **Deferred proofs**: Explicitly marked in [Proof Status](theory/proofs.md); citations are not treated as proofs.
- If an exposition is found to be inconsistent with the textbooks, the standard textbook formulation takes precedence, and the discrepancy is noted on the page.
